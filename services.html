Services

Six disciplines. One hydraulics-first approach.

M3 provides engineering planning, design, construction services and LEED support for public and private clients across civil and environmental engineering.

01  /  Discipline
Capabilities

Water & wastewater

Collection system engineering from the first alignment study through startup, with the hydraulic modeling to prove the design works before anything is built.

  • Alignment studies
  • Sewer design
  • Inflow and infiltration reduction
  • Sanitary Sewer Evaluation Study (SSES)
  • Hydraulic modeling: SWMM, SewerGEMS, HYDRA
  • Condition assessment
  • Combined sewer separation
  • Private inflow reduction
  • Pump station design
  • Force main design

02  /  Discipline
Capabilities

Stormwater

Watershed-scale planning and structure-scale design, built on hydrologic and hydraulic models we run in house rather than subcontract.

  • Storm sewer system design
  • Master planning
  • Floodplain analysis
  • Hydraulic modeling: SWMM, HEC-RAS, HEC-HMS, HEC-GeoRAS
  • HEC-RAS 1D and 2D
  • Water quality modeling and design
  • Sediment transport
  • Hydrologic design
  • Bridge and culvert hydraulics
  • Permitting: 404, 401, floodplain

03  /  Discipline
Capabilities

Stream restoration

We start with the geomorphology. Identifying why a bank is failing is what makes the fix hold, and it is the work most often skipped.

  • Bio-stabilization and restoration design
  • Geomorphic assessment
  • 401 and 404 permitting
  • 2D and 3D hydraulic modeling
  • Mitigation design
  • GIS streambank assessment

07  /  Discipline
Capabilities

CCTV sewer inspection

Closed-circuit television inspections that turn field observations into defensible sewer condition data, I/I findings and rehabilitation priorities. Our field lead and three of his staff are NASSCO certified.

  • CCTV inspection planning and field coordination
  • Sewer cleaning
  • Condition assessment for mains, structures and manholes
  • Custom GIS maps and client-specific report formats
  • Smoke testing and manhole scans
  • I/I source identification
  • Prioritised rehabilitation recommendations
  • Capital planning and SSES reporting
