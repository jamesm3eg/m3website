Careers

We would rather train the right engineer than hire the safe one.

M3 runs an internal training program, finances external training, and gives every employee paid time each month to volunteer. If you want to do hydraulics properly, this is a good place to do it.

Working here

Small firm, real responsibility, no drafting-room purgatory.

An engineering firm is only as good as its employees. We look for people who are highly motivated and well trained, with the potential to become some of the best professionals in the industry.

We run M3 University for internal training and we finance external training too. We reward hard work, and we take work-life balance seriously — including eight hours of paid volunteer leave every month.
