Where we go deep

Most of our work starts with a model and ends underground.

Our staff specialises in complex hydraulic modeling of storm and sanitary systems, risk-based flood mitigation, and stream biostabilization. That analytical core runs through everything else we do — roadway and bridge design, condition assessment, GIS and asset management, LEED support, and construction administration.

Selected work

A register of the projects we are known for.

Clients include the Metropolitan St. Louis Sewer District, IDOT, MoDOT, the City of St. Louis, St. Louis County, Great Rivers Greenway and Illinois American Water.

WW / 02

Creve Coeur Creek Sanitary, Phase VI

City of Chesterfield, MO

Wastewater

7,933 linear feet of sanitary sewer from 10″ to 54″ in diameter, including three stream crossings with rip-rap protection over the piping.

SW / 02

Comprehensive Stormwater Master Plan

City of St. Charles, MO

Stormwater

Master plan and economic analysis across 20 square miles, recommending flood mitigation measures and prioritising stream restoration.

SR / 03

Gravois Creek Stream Stabilization

Metropolitan St. Louis Sewer District

Stream restoration

Geomorphic assessment of eight miles of creek where bank erosion had exposed large-diameter sanitary sewers, using M3’s GIS streambank assessment tool.

AM / 01

Forest Park Asset Inventory & Management

City of St. Louis

Asset management

6,000 assets surveyed and 120,000 data points uploaded, with condition assessment tools built to calculate remaining useful life.

TR / 03

JS McDonnell Boulevard Bridge Replacement

St. Louis County

Transportation

Replacement design for a functionally obsolete three-span bridge over Coldwater Creek carrying nearly 15,000 vehicles a day near Lambert Airport.

CM / 03

Frontenac Pump Station & Force Main

Village of Godfrey, IL

Construction mgmt

1,200 feet of force main, a new pump station and valve vault, 300,000 gallons of backup storage and a backup generator, under an EPA consent decree.
