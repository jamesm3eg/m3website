Marjorie Melton, PE, LEED AP

She is the former President of the St. Louis Board of Public Service, where she led a staff of over 220 and a combined operating and capital budget of more than $65 million.

Principal

Marc Eshelman, PE

LinkedIn ↗

Mr. Eshelman has 30 years of experience in roadway, site development, stormwater, and water and wastewater design, study and management. His project management work covers contract negotiation, scheduling, technical supervision and client communication. He works across a wide range of hydrologic and hydraulic software — HEC-1, HEC-2, HEC-6, HEC-HMS, HEC-RAS, WSPRO, Pond Pack, XP-SWMM, TR-20, AdICPR and the Cybernet water distribution model. He currently teaches the Hydrology and Hydraulics PE Review Course for the Missouri Society of Professional Engineers.
