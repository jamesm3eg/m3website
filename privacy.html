Privacy Policy

QuickBooks Online Expense Integration · Effective [DATE]

This policy describes how the QuickBooks Online expense integration (the “Application”) operated by M3 Engineering Group, P.C. (“M3”) accesses, uses, stores, and deletes data. The Application is internal software used only by M3 personnel against M3’s own QuickBooks Online company file.

1Scope

The Application is not a consumer product and is not offered to the public. It has no end-user accounts, no sign-up flow, and no public interface. It does not connect to QuickBooks Online companies belonging to any organization other than M3.

2Data the Application accesses

Through Intuit’s published APIs, and only under the accounting scope authorized by an M3 QuickBooks Online administrator, the Application reads and writes:

  • expense and purchase transactions, including date, amount, memo, and account coding;
  • vendor and payee records;
  • chart of accounts entries;
  • customer and project references used for cost allocation.

The Application does not access payroll records, employee personal data, bank account or card numbers, tax identification numbers, or payment credentials.

3How the data is used

Data is used for one purpose: reconciling corporate credit card expense records between M3’s project management system and M3’s QuickBooks Online company file, including assigning the correct payee to imported transactions. It is not used for analytics, profiling, advertising, model training, or any secondary purpose.

4Storage and security

The Application processes transaction data in memory and does not maintain a persistent copy of accounting records outside QuickBooks Online. OAuth tokens and client credentials are stored encrypted at rest on M3-controlled infrastructure with access limited to authorized IT personnel. All communication with Intuit endpoints uses TLS. Application logs record operational events and do not retain transaction detail.

5Sharing

M3 does not sell, rent, or share data accessed by the Application with any third party. Data is transmitted only between M3-controlled systems and Intuit. No subprocessors receive QuickBooks data from the Application.

6Retention and deletion

Operational logs are retained for no more than 90 days and then deleted. OAuth tokens are deleted when the connection is revoked. Disconnecting the Application from QuickBooks Online — either from within QuickBooks Online or by request to M3 — causes M3 to revoke and delete all stored tokens and cease all API access. Accounting records themselves remain in QuickBooks Online and are governed by M3’s records retention practices.

7Cookies and tracking

The Application sets no cookies and performs no tracking. This page is served as static content and does not collect visitor information.

8Children

The Application is business software and is not directed to, or usable by, anyone under 18.

M3 may revise this policy. The effective date above reflects the most recent revision.
