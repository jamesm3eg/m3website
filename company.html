About the firm

Founded in 2009 to do the hydraulics other firms send out.

M3 Engineering Group, P.C. is a women- and minority-owned consulting firm with offices in St. Louis, St. Charles and Chicago. We provide engineering, asset management, planning, design and LEED support for public and private clients across civil and environmental engineering.

Who we are

A small firm built around a deep technical core.

M3 was founded in St. Louis in 2009. We are certified as a Minority and Woman-owned Disadvantaged Business Enterprise, and we work both as a prime consultant and as a specialist partner to larger firms.

Our staff specialises in complex hydraulic modeling of storm and sanitary systems, risk-based flood mitigation, and stream biostabilization. Those are the problems agencies call us about first — and the reason we get asked to stay on for design, permitting and construction.

Core values

How do we make a difference?

Four commitments, written down when the firm was founded and still the basis of how we hire, review and reward.

01

In our industry

We believe in exceeding client expectations by delivering a high quality product at a fair price. A structured quality control process and solid accounting practices are part of our management approach.

02

In our environment

Our staff has technical expertise in stream biostabilization, rain garden design and sustainable construction materials. We run an internal sustainability program to reduce our own carbon footprint, and we help clients do the same.

03

In our community

Every employee gets eight hours of paid leave each month for community service. We have built houses for Habitat for Humanity, cleaned Missouri rivers through Operation Clean Stream, collected food through Operation Food Search, and mentored aspiring engineers at Gateway High School.

04

In the lives of our employees

An engineering firm is only as good as its people. We run M3 University for internal training and finance external training as well. We reward hard work, protect time with family, and take work-life balance seriously.
