Project index

Twenty-five projects, seven disciplines, one region.

Filter by discipline. Every entry below is work M3 led or delivered as a specialist partner — for sewer districts, DOTs, cities, counties, parks departments and utilities.

WW / 01

SCADA System Upgrade

Metropolitan St. Louis Sewer District

Wastewater

MSD needed to relocate several hundred SCADA towers serving sanitary lift stations for a new region-wide system. M3 investigated candidate sites using GIS and designed fiber optic lines beneath railroads and highways.

WW / 02

Creve Coeur Creek Sanitary, Phase VI

City of Chesterfield, MO

Wastewater

7,933 linear feet of sanitary sewer from 10″ to 54″ in diameter, west of Schoettler Drive and north of Clayton Road, including three stream crossings with rip-rap protection over the piping.

WW / 03

Alternate Flood Control Measures Analysis

Chicago, IL

Wastewater

A multi-year assignment updating and running the Chicago TARP model in InfoWorks, then using optimisation software to weigh the benefits and impacts of alternate flood control measures across subareas. Included stakeholder workshops and presentation materials.

WW / 04

DC-02 and DC-03 Sanitary Sewer Design

Metropolitan St. Louis Sewer District

Wastewater

6,000 feet of 48″ relief sewer designed to help eliminate constructed SSOs under MSD’s consent decree with the EPA. Work began by evaluating alternative alignments from Two Mile Creek to Log Cabin.

SW / 01

Flood Mitigation Analysis

Village of Harwood Heights, IL

Stormwater

The village had suffered frequent flooding that inundated streets and damaged buildings. M3 used XP-SWMM to analyse the causes and develop candidate solutions.

SW / 02

Comprehensive Stormwater Master Plan

City of St. Charles, MO

Stormwater

Master plan and economic analysis across 20 square miles, recommending flood mitigation measures, prioritising stream restoration and identifying steps to improve water quality.

SW / 03

Bridle Spur Culvert

St. Louis County, MO

Stormwater

An existing 785-foot corrugated metal pipe culvert, 66″ to 102″ in diameter, had rusted through along much of its flowline. The entrance failed during a heavy rain event and required emergency repair.

SW / 04

Force Main Condition Assessment and Rehabilitation

Maryland Heights, MO

Stormwater

Following complaints of flooding along Essex Creek, a tributary to Midland Creek, M3 performed the hydrologic analysis together with a geomorphic investigation of the stream to determine the most effective measures.

SW / 05

Cole Creek Flood Reduction

City of St. Charles, MO

Stormwater

Alternatives analysis and design to alleviate flooding of homes and apartments along several miles of Cole Creek, as part of a multifaceted city-committed project.

SR / 01

Various Culvert Replacements

St. Louis County, MO

Stream restoration

Replacement of three box culverts — Villa Dorado Drive (11′h × 15′w), Lackland Road (6′h × 12′w) and Lockwood Avenue (12′w × 8′h). M3 performed the floodplain studies.

SR / 02

Maline Creek Bank Stabilization

Great Rivers Greenway District

Stream restoration

Two miles of greenway trail and three pedestrian bridges along Maline Creek, with a rain garden and stream biostabilization at three locations to ensure the new bridges would be supported.

SR / 03

Gravois Creek Stream Stabilization

Metropolitan St. Louis Sewer District

Stream restoration

Geomorphic assessment of eight miles of creek to evaluate bank stability adjacent to a proposed sanitary storage tank and several large-diameter sewers that erosion had exposed. Began with M3’s GIS streambank assessment tool.

SR / 04

Dougherty Lake Shoreline Protection

St. Louis County Parks Department

Stream restoration

Shoreline erosion at a county-owned lake was threatening residential back yards. M3 traced the loss back to the 1980s and designed a protection solution.

AM / 01

Forest Park Asset Inventory and Management

City of St. Louis

Asset management

Data collected on 6,000 assets including buildings, statues, roads, sidewalks, bridges and park amenities. 24 data collection applications built in ArcInfo, 120,000 data points uploaded to ArcMap, and condition assessment tools developed to calculate remaining useful life.

AM / 02

St. Louis ADA Transition Plan

City of St. Louis (sub to Oates)

Asset management

Evaluation of physical barriers limiting access for people with disabilities across programs, services and activities in the city-maintained public right-of-way.

AM / 03

Airport Facilities Condition Assessment

St. Louis Lambert International Airport

Asset management

Inspection and condition assessment of Terminals 1 and 2, Concourses A through E, the east and west climate control facilities, adjacent parking garages and the east and west garages.

TR / 01

4th Street and Broadway Sidewalks and Overlay

City of St. Louis

Transportation

Corridor evaluation and improvements along 4th Street and Broadway from Chouteau to Convention Plaza — one of the most heavily used corridors in downtown St. Louis, with several historic and iconic buildings.

TR / 02

PTB 197 Various Phase II Projects

Illinois DOT (sub to Wood)

Transportation

Phase II engineering for road and bridge improvements across various routes and counties in Region One / District One, including contract plan preparation, specifications and estimates, and value engineering.

TR / 03

JS McDonnell Boulevard Bridge Replacement

St. Louis County, MO

Transportation

Replacement design for a functionally obsolete three-span bridge carrying nearly 15,000 vehicles a day over Coldwater Creek, providing vital access to businesses north of Lambert Airport.

TR / 04

Lindbergh Boulevard Tunnel Inspection

St. Louis Lambert International Airport

Transportation

Periodic inspection of the Lindbergh Boulevard tunnel beneath airport runways, under the National Tunnel Inspection Standards established by FHWA in 2015.

TR / 05

Old Halls Ferry Bridge Replacement

St. Louis County, MO

Transportation

Replacement of the Old Halls Ferry bridge over Halls Ferry Creek. M3 performed the floodplain study to confirm the proposed structure would not cause an adverse impact.

CM / 01

La Vista Park Sewer Protection

Illinois American Water

Construction mgmt

1,500 feet of existing sanitary sewer near a pump station in La Vista Park, Godfrey, IL was threatened by a meandering stream with failing banks that had exposed the sewer in several places.

CM / 02

Deer Creek Park Streambank Repairs

City of Maplewood, MO

Construction mgmt

M3 acted as owner’s representative for inspection and contractor compliance, pay applications, shop drawing review, startup and closeout on 405 feet of streambank stabilised with stone toe protection and live staking.

CM / 03

Frontenac Pump Station and Force Main

Village of Godfrey, IL

Construction mgmt

Part of a system under EPA consent decree: 1,200 feet of force main, a new pump station and valve vault, 300,000 gallons of backup storage and a new backup generator.

CM / 04

Streambank Program (B)

Metropolitan St. Louis Sewer District

Construction mgmt

Design and construction administration for nine separate streambank projects across the St. Louis region, ranging from 400 to 1,200 feet, alleviating erosion affecting residential rear yards.
