NASSCO-certified capacity

Four NASSCO-certified staff on the field team.

M3's field services group carries four NASSCO certifications, including the group lead. Certification isn't a formality here — it's how condition data stays consistent and defensible from the first setup to the final report.

The team coordinates CCTV, sewer cleaning, manhole scans, custom GIS deliverables and report formats — so the people planning the inspection are the people answerable for the data that comes back.

M3 services

Inspection is only useful if the report can carry a decision.

Water and wastewater engineering and asset management sit under one roof here, so field data is shaped by the people who will use it in a hydraulic model or a capital plan.

  • Field data shaped into custom GIS and reporting formats for client decisions.
  • Extensive experience with hydraulic modeling, condition assessment and I/I reduction.
  • CCTV inspection planning, field coordination and sewer cleaning.
  • Condition assessment documentation for sanitary sewer mains, structures and manholes.
  • Custom GIS maps and report formats tailored to each client's needs.
  • Smoke testing, manhole scans and I/I source identification to complement CCTV findings.
  • Prioritised recommendations for rehabilitation, capital planning and SSES reporting.
